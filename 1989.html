SIKABOTA FIG TREE HOME 1989

Tanzania
In 1989 they all had a chance to see each other in Tanzania. Even bringing new friends from Denmark.

The late Dixon was struggling in Nairobi, and Birgit was working in Tanzania these years. She invited us all to Dar Es Salaam.

These where good days, going to the beach and going on safari in this grand country.

As grownups this was the time for Jesper, Line and Dixon to reflect on the past experiences together. Not realizing it would be another 25 years until they would see each other again, only months before Dixon would pass away at the age of 51.
